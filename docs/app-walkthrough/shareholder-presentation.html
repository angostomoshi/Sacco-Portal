Shareholder Walkthrough

Metropolitan Hospital Sacco Member Portal

A visual walkthrough of the web app: member login, dashboard, profile, M-Pesa payments, statements, loans, dividends, guarantors, and account self-service.

Captured using member MS041 for demonstration. No payment, loan application, account creation, password change, or PDF download was submitted.

Secure Member Login

Members access the portal using their member number and password.

Dashboard Overview

The dashboard summarizes balances, member status, loan exposure, and the most important member actions.

Member Profile

Members can confirm personal details used for statements, payment prompts, and Sacco communication.

Deposit Via M-Pesa

Members choose whether a payment is for savings, withdrawable deposits, or loan repayment before approving the phone prompt.

Savings Statement

The report shows recent savings transactions, including successful M-Pesa savings payments after Daraja callback confirmation.

Withdrawable Statement

Withdrawable deposits are shown separately from member savings to protect reporting accuracy.

Loan Statement

Members can view active loans, interest, monthly repayment, outstanding balance, and repayment options.

Instant Loan Application

The portal previews repayment values before a member submits an application.

Dividends

Members can view dividend information and related shareholder benefits.

Guarantors

The guarantor view gives members visibility into loans where they appear as guarantors.

Share Capital

The share capital report shows ownership contribution separately from savings and withdrawable deposits.

Account Creation

Members can create portal access where self-onboarding is enabled.

Password Change

Members can update credentials through a self-service password flow.

Shareholder Value

The portal improves transparency, reduces manual follow-up, and gives members direct access to balances, reports, loan information, guarantor exposure, dividends, and M-Pesa payment services.

TransparencyMembers can view balances, reports, loans, dividends, and guarantees from one place.
Operational EfficiencyDigital self-service reduces routine office requests and manual statement follow-up.
Payment ConfidenceM-Pesa payments are tied to member, loan, and deposit references for cleaner ledger posting.
